# Display Lab — Tested Monitor Setups

Multi-monitor setups on Windows vary widely in resolution, DPI scaling, relative positioning (negative coordinates left of or above the primary monitor), and docking behavior.

Because Quboid collects no telemetry, this table is maintained from community reports. If Quboid works (or breaks!) on your setup, [share your display setup](https://github.com/SormanoS/quboid/issues/new?template=display_setup.yml) or post in [Discussions](https://github.com/SormanoS/quboid/discussions).
